<p align="center"><b>No chat box. Talk to Moe from any app on your Mac, and it talks back.</b></p>

Memoe (say it like "memo") is a Mac app. Hold a key in whatever app you're in, say what you need, and Moe answers out loud. It remembers what matters about you, and you can read, correct or delete any of it on the Memory page. It can do the work on your Mac and in the services you connect, and it asks before anything goes out in your name.

It runs on the AI account you already have, Claude or ChatGPT, or on any provider's key, or on a model on your own Mac.

- **Download:** [memoe.app](https://memoe.app)
- **Docs:** [docs.memoe.app](https://docs.memoe.app)

Memoe is built on [Hermes Agent](https://github.com/NousResearch/hermes-agent) by Nous Research.
